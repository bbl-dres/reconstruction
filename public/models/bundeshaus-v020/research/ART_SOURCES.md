The 2021 BBL programme resolves the northern allegories, seated niche figures, historians and griffins. The heritage and parliamentary inventories identify the south professions and principal hall/chamber sculptures. The local encyclopedia is used as a placement/attribution lead when the primary text is incomplete; those records remain medium confidence.

The restoration architect identifies the small Berna allegories as cast iron (Grauguss), correcting the bronze description in the secondary encyclopedia. Their model uses a simplified pale painted finish. Precise season-to-position mapping is unknown. The courtyard position uses the published CH1903 coordinate and the project terrain, not a survey; height is fitted to the existing terrain (z=0.433 m).
