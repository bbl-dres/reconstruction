# Bundeshaus v010

Imported from the frozen v010 3D model and its stage09 viewer handoff. This iteration adds the south public entrance beneath the National Council, including the lobby and arcade, revolving doors, glass screens, ceiling discs, paving, reception furniture and two stair flights. Matching terrain/context is cut locally to expose the new space.

The building contains 9,341 placements and 2,519 shared mesh definitions. Its prepared collection inventory and IDs are retained by lossless Meshopt/gzip delivery. Select **South public entrance** in Dollhouse or Floor plan to inspect the new −4.20 m level, or use its saved point of interest. Door units are static and the entrance is outside the author's tested walking coverage.

Later milestones refine these areas. See the [modeling performance requirements](../../../docs/model-handoff.md#geometry-materials-and-performance).
