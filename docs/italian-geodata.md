# Finding Italian terrain, roofs, parcels and land cover

[New reconstruction guide](adding-a-building.md) · [Elevation sources](elevation-sources.md) · [Terrain workflow](terrain-and-surroundings.md)

Checked 8 October 2026. Start with the actual municipality/region and a small area of interest. Use the Italian [RNDT catalogue](https://geodati.gov.it/) to find the publisher and live service. The [European Commission confirms](https://knowledge-base.inspire.ec.europa.eu/news-and-publications/news/european-commission-transitioning-inspire-infrastructure-eu-horizontal-services-ensure-long-term-2026-07-01_en) that the INSPIRE Geoportal was retired in July 2026, with discovery moving to [data.europa.eu](https://data.europa.eu/). The Knowledge Base still contains useful specifications and some old redirecting links.

| Need | Search for | Check before modelling |
|---|---|---|
| Ground elevation | DTM, modello digitale del terreno, punti quotati, curve di livello | Vertical datum, native spacing, acquisition date, actual valid pixels and ground control |
| Roof/tree elevation | DSM, DSM FIRST, LiDAR, nuvola di punti | Coverage at the site; trees can hide roofs; raw/classified data and licences may differ from the viewing service |
| Roof geometry | DBGT/DBT, elemento di copertura, falde, unità volumetrica | Geometry must retain Z or explicit height attributes; sparse roof-detail layers may omit entire buildings |
| Parcels and building outlines | Agenzia delle Entrate, cartografia catastale, particelle, fabbricati | WMS images are not vector downloads, parcel labels are not ownership, and displayed pixels are not survey precision |
| Surface/vegetation classes | Carta di Uso del Suolo (CUS), land cover, CORINE | Minimum mapping area and date; regional classification cannot locate individual garden trees or paving edges |

The [Agenzia delle Entrate WMS](https://wms.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/inspire/wms/ows01.php?service=WMS&version=1.3.0&request=GetCapabilities) was anonymously accessible in this audit and declares CC BY 4.0. `CP.CadastralParcel`, `fabbricati` and `codice_plla` provide a useful map; GetFeatureInfo can provide an identifier. A GML response may contain only a bounding box. The separate [free map-sheet service](https://unicat.agenziaentrate.gov.it/cgconsade/dist/img/Consultazione%20dei%20fogli%20di%20mappa%20catastale_Manuale_Utente_20251107.pdf) requires SPID/CIE/CNS login according to its official manual; a human collaborator can handle that route. Do not confuse anonymous viewing with authenticated extraction.

For Lazio, the [regional Geoportal](https://geoportale.regione.lazio.it/) exposes GeoNode metadata and GeoServer services. The scoped WFS capabilities at `/geoserver/geonode/wfs` worked when the global request failed. Discover exact layer names from capabilities/API; inspect the response body, because HTTP 200 may contain an OGC error. Save exact queries, hashes, CRS and returned counts under private `work/research/geodata/`.

Villa Maraini was a useful counterexample to catalogue-only discovery: a working DBGT roof layer returned three nearby 3D polygons, **none over the villa**; national 1 m/2 m DSM services returned NoData there. Cadastral mapping succeeded. These are site-specific results, not claims that Italy lacks roof data. Preserve the source Z datum as unknown until verified, and never replace missing heights with an undocumented assumption. The owner-authorized 10 m context default remains explicitly approximate.

Photogrammetric Google Earth views belong under `research/geospatial-imagery/`, not the architectural evidence manifest. Record screenshot date separately from underlying imagery date. Use primary photographs and calibrated drawings to confirm visible features; map-render distortions cannot establish dimensions. For a complete regional extract or unpublished survey, prepare product names, bbox, CRS and missing metadata for a human request rather than repeatedly trying unavailable services.

The separately tested [Lazio 2 m DTM](https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/m_amte-299fn3-6636776d-0c1e-4e48-c094-d0c256bafec8?locale=en), layer `EL.LIDAR.LAZIO.2x2.DTM`, has coastal-strip coverage in the broader Rome test, unlike the 1 m river-corridor product. It also returns NoData at Villa Maraini. Check DTM and DSM independently; equal resolution or similar catalogue envelopes do not establish equal coverage.

The [RNDT collection on data.europa.eu](https://data.europa.eu/data/catalogues/rndt?locale=en) is another discovery entry point supplied by the owner. Avoid a catalogue-wide keyword trawl: use publisher, region, product type and the local bbox. For the [Lazio 1 m DTM record](https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/m_amte-299fn3-b86e27e3-479d-4ec3-d17f-9883bd0495c2?locale=en), the live `EL.LIDAR.LAZIO.1x1.DTM` layer rendered central-Rome river-corridor coverage but returned NoData at Villa Maraini. A broader positive-control map is useful when a local query is empty; it distinguishes a site gap from a wholly failing service. Do not interpret the EU extent rectangle as complete regional coverage.

**Official aerial imagery:** [AGEA 2020 regional metadata](https://geoportale.regione.lazio.it/catalogue/csw_to_extra_format/r_lazio%3A92b0f7e3-3b75-4feb-ac5e-2382/ortofoto-agea-v-2020.html) records a 2020 flight, 2023 publication, EPSG:25833 and CC BY 4.0. Roma Capitale's advertised `/geoserver/ows` WMS layer `REGLAZ:2020_AGEA_25833_COG` returned a useful image at Villa Maraini. Save exact GetMap bbox, CRS, dimensions and pixel-centre worldfile. A requested 0.30 m pixel spacing is not proof of native resolution. Metadata flags and actual service accessibility can disagree; record the tested result, and distinguish a map image from raw imagery or height data.

Check older and newer **survey campaigns**, not just catalogue update dates. The Lazio 1 m record's 2008–2011 acquisition, 2013 publication and 2026 catalogue refresh are separate facts. Rome's [public services](https://geoportale.comune.roma.it/servizi/) yielded a useful 2003 building-outline layer, but its name `CostruzioniVolumiCTRN` did not imply height data: the tested export was 2D. The city's [official procurement Q&A](https://appalti.comune.roma.it/gare/dettaglio.php?codice=361) confirms an October 2022 municipal flight, while the [GeoRoma programme](https://www.comune.roma.it/web/it/progetti-pnrr-e-altri-fondi-straordinari-pn-metro-plus-21-27.page?contentId=PRG1163632) provides a newer acquisition lead. Do not present planned terrain/3D products as published downloads. A dated building-specific historic survey may be more useful than a recent coarse national model.
