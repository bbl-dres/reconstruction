Building & site

Bern’s Parliament Building (opens in a new tab), designed by Hans Wilhelm Auer and completed in 1902, stands between the Federal Palace’s east and west wings. In front, Bundesplatz (opens in a new tab) has 26 water jets representing Switzerland’s cantons.

Move around

Mouse
Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. In Floor plan, drag to pan.
Touch
One finger rotates; two fingers pan or pinch to zoom. In Floor plan, one finger pans.
Keyboard
Tab to focus the viewer. Arrows rotate, Shift + arrows pan, + / − zoom, R fits. In Floor plan, arrows pan.
Walking
WASD/arrows move, mouse looks, Shift speeds up, Space jumps, Esc pauses. On touch screens, use the movement pad and drag to look.

Explore building opens saved Places and searchable Elements. Select an element in Exterior, Dollhouse or Floor plan to inspect its properties and available sources.
