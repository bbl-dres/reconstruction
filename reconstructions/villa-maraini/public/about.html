Villa Maraini · Archive architectural study

The main villa of the Istituto Svizzero in Rome, reconstructed from archival drawings and reviewed against photographs. Experimental study, October 2026.

Version 14 comes from a native Revit model. Explore five principal villa storeys and two tower levels, with walls aligned per facade plane, window and door families, LOD 300 facade decoration, stairs, balustrades and rooms from the functional booklet. Select an element to read its sources, method and tolerances. The Portineria, the street gate, the Dependance, the garden and the neighbourhood form the surroundings, which can be hidden or muted to put the villa in focus.

The model combines historical evidence and does not represent verified current conditions. Regional 5 m terrain and nearby buildings provide approximate context. Neighbour heights combine OpenStreetMap information, GlobalBuildingAtlas estimates and Google Earth readings. Geographic registration (about ±0.5 m) and the floor-to-ground tie remain provisional.

Terrain adapted from Regione Lazio, CTRN 2002, tile 374062, CC BY 4.0. Roads and building footprints © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Data clipped, transformed and triangulated; some storey-based heights inferred.

Neighbouring height predictions: GlobalBuildingAtlas, Zhu et al. (2025), CC BY-NC 4.0. Selected estimates extruded over existing bases. Local experimental context; commercial reuse is not cleared.
