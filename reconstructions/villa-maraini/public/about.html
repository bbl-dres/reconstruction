Villa Maraini · Archive architectural study

The main villa of the Istituto Svizzero in Rome, reconstructed from archival floor plans and a historical section. First study, October 2026.

Explore five principal storeys and the tower. Major walls follow calibrated drawings; some thin partitions are missing. Openings, stairs and roof details are provisional. Decorative interiors, the garden, Portineria and Dependance are not reconstructed.

The model combines historical evidence and does not represent verified current conditions. The site study adds a 5 m terrain grid, mapped roads and nearby building footprints. 99 neighbouring masses use GlobalBuildingAtlas height estimates, five use storey counts and one retains a 10 m placeholder. Geographic alignment is approximate and the floor-to-ground tie is provisional. Read the model status and limitations.
