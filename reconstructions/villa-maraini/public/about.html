Villa Maraini · Archive architectural study

The main villa of the Istituto Svizzero in Rome, reconstructed from archival drawings and reviewed against photographs. Experimental study, October 2026.

Explore five principal villa storeys, two tower levels and the Portineria's three separate levels. Reviewed wall cores, window families and glazed doors follow calibrated plans and photographs. The ceremonial stair includes the central descent to the lower vestibule. The garden has separate stairs, paving and retaining construction, including the winding frontage stair and historical carriageway. The Portineria's upper garden connection, full upper drive return, exact ornament, planting and some hidden circulation remain unresolved.

The model combines historical evidence and does not represent verified current conditions. Regional 5 m terrain, mapped streets and nearby buildings provide approximate context, including courtyards and stepped building parts. Neighbor heights combine OpenStreetMap information and GlobalBuildingAtlas estimates. Geographic alignment and the floor-to-ground tie remain provisional. Read the model status and limitations.
