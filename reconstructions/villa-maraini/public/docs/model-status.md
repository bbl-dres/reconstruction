# Villa Maraini — v013 audit release

v013 is the remediation release of a multidisciplinary audit (historian, survey, architect, engineer and BIM lenses, twelve review topics, 278 findings) of the v012 model. It keeps the v012 street, context and registered site work and adds bounded, evidence-led corrections: the p1 basement enclosure is closed at its south-west corner; the service flights now land on the slabs; the p5 rooms have their ceiling slab from the historic section chain; a straight service flight continues from p5 to the roof-access level and spiral stairs connect the tower levels and its terrace (placeholders at the booklet positions); two documented lift/service shafts, the booklet's thin partitions on p2/p3 and interior door products of shared inferred types are added; the loggia floor, the vestibule doorway, the altana door head and the court retaining core are corrected; ground voids at the stair feet are closed; the neighbouring masses are moved onto the official municipal outlines.

At the owner's instruction (2026-10-10 revision) the lodge, the arrival wall and the Via Ludovisi wall stand in one plane and the main carriage gate stands diagonally across the Via Ludovisi/Via Cadore corner facing the drive, with a pedestrian gate in each street wall; the carriage drive is one continuous road: gate, Via Cadore lane, a climbing ramp inside the Via Liguria retaining head, and a garden road along the villa's east side into the entrance court. The garden plateau is flat to the perimeter walls, whose crowns are level; outside its western and southern edge a lower walk and apron (about two metres down, HMQ level) run along the Via Ludovisi and south walls with stairs between the levels, and the walk meets the lodge terrace by a flight. The neighbouring former stables are an independent pair of buildings (roof 63 m, terrace 60 m, courtyard 53 m from Google Earth). The undated garden plan was used for positions only; levels come from the HMQ section, the municipal retaining-head line, the modelled lawn and the Google Earth readings under the provisional display offset. These are placeholders snapped to the modelled walls, court and drive. The tall garden trees (eight umbrella pines, the Phoenix palm beside the entrance court, three tall fan palms, a cypress pair and two Phoenix palms by the pond lawn) are placed from the owner's Google Earth canopy readings, the 2020 orthophoto and garden photographs; their heights follow the canopy readings under the provisional display offset and their positions are placeholders within a few metres.

The BIM registry now groups stair treads into flights and railing pieces into runs, contains site elements in the site, shares types for identical geometry, records an opening for every window and door, and carries wall thickness, type and axis properties plus level membership derived from the occupied storey bands. Every added piece is marked `inferred` with the audit finding it answers.

Open, by owner decision or for the next rounds: the absolute vertical tie (three readings put the piano rialzato about 1.9 m higher in the official height system than the provisional display offset), the gate dimensions and the exact chord position across the corner (inherited, not measured), the Portineria plan width and street tie, the tower cap height and roof urns, and the interior bearing-wall axis re-trace. Dimensions remain a cross-date archive study, not a measured survey; see the release report and [site data credits](site-data-credits.md).
