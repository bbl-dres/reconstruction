# Villa Maraini — v003 archive and site study

Local study, 8 October 2026. v003 preserves the v001 main villa (five principal storeys plus two tower levels), v002 terrain and roads, and improves inferred heights for 99 neighbouring masses. Editable scripted Blender source and GLB/BIM/IFC reference delivery.

Plans come from the functional occupancy booklet (PDF exported in 2005; underlying survey date unconfirmed). Relative levels come from a historical existing-condition section. The 1994 and 2024 drawings corroborate floor identity but show changed uses/partitions. This is not a verified 2024 or present-day model.

- Major walls and exterior opening positions follow calibrated vector drawings. Some thin line-only partitions remain untraced.
- Graphic scale checks differ by at most 3.4 mm; this is a drawing check, not survey accuracy. Initial wall extraction tolerance is approximately 4.5 cm; subsequent hatch-artifact cleanup is bounded to 7 cm from that contour. Cross-floor alignment has a 39.8 cm held-out discrepancy; adopt 45 cm study uncertainty.
- Piano rialzato is local z=0. Approximate horizontal OSM footprint registration has 1.02 m boundary RMS, without rescaling architecture. The absolute datum and measured floor tie remain unresolved. Sun and sky are unavailable.
- Opening head heights, glazing proxies, simple edge bands and stair flights are inferred. Door/window type, sill height, joinery, curved heads and ornate facade/interior details remain to be matched to photographs/elevations.
- The main roof is an inferred flat study at local z16.32, accommodating the section's higher 4.70 m room crown; stepped roofs, slopes and stair-head cover are unresolved.
- Terrain uses Regione Lazio's 2002 5 m DTM, covering roughly 600 m. Ground-to-floor placement assumes rialzato 0.50 m above a nearby terrain-ring median; this is not a measured floor elevation. The building cutout has 0.20 m display clearance. Basement exposure and the wall/ground junction are provisional.
- Surroundings include 189 mapped road/path ways with inferred widths and 105 building footprints. 99 neighbours use footprint-matched GlobalBuildingAtlas height estimates; five use OSM storey counts × assumed 3 m/storey; one retains the owner-requested 10 m placeholder after failing footprint matching. All context heights and flat roofs are inferred. Garden details, retaining walls, Portineria and Dependance architecture are not reconstructed. GBA heights carry a noncommercial licence; see the credits before reuse.
- Stairs are provisional and full walking routes have not been certified. Upper tower spiral stair and guardrails are missing.
- No source photographs, artwork or archive drawings are reproduced in the public model. Materials are neutral presentation colours.

The regional map's 65.4 m villa-ground spot height and nearby 54–56 m streets corroborate the selected terrain. TINITALY 10 m data differ by about 19 m locally and are not used in the model; the reason remains unproven. This is a map consistency check, not independent survey validation.
