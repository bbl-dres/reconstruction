# Villa Maraini — research status

**8 October 2026 · Phase 1: evidence gathering · No model release**

Villa Maraini is the Rome home of the Istituto Svizzero at Via Ludovisi 48. The gallery point **41.906773, 12.487714 (WGS84)** comes from [Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/node/2150); it is a visitor-location pin, not survey control.

## Initial findings

- [HMQ](https://gebaeudeaufnahme.ch/referenzen/5/villa-maraini.html) documents a 2012 survey for BBL, including terrain. Its [reference sheet](https://gebaeudeaufnahme.ch/media/uploads/references/5/documents/rom-villa-maraini-referenzblatt-hmq.pdf) contains a plan, section, elevation and façade photograph. It has been visually inspected; dimensioned originals and calibration are still needed.
- [BBL's index](https://www.bbl.admin.ch/fr/concours) links the [2013 refurbishment jury publication](https://online.fliphtml5.com/wqmpj/vsjp/). The full PDF is saved privately; selected site and winning-project spreads have been inspected, including multi-level plans, elevations and sections. Competition proposals are not proof of construction.
- [Edilerica](https://www.edilerica.it/progetti/villa-maraini/) describes entrance and Sala Elvetica alterations in 2022–2023. Their executed extent and current layout need verification.
- The [institute](https://www.istitutosvizzero.it/istituto-svizzero/) supplies imagery and guided visits by appointment. No reusable 360° dataset has been confirmed.

## Next evidence

Priorities: the 2012 measured survey, recent as-built drawings, dated interior/exterior photographs, and site/height control. Architectural/BIM modelling is the working direction; splat feasibility needs overlapping imagery and reuse rights.

Downloads, coverage and source records are held privately in `work/`. No geometry or assumed floor heights have been published. Target state and detailed scope remain provisional.
