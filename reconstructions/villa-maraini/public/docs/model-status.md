# Villa Maraini — v011 architectural study

The model includes straight BIM wall cores, symmetric controlled reveals, reusable complete window/door/column products and transparent glazing. The central eleven-riser descent, lower vestibule and three distinct glazed door assemblies follow the reviewed plans and photographs. Portineria now has its own three levels, curved stair bay, open loggia and roofs.

The v010 winding garden stair, gate approach, perimeter carriageway and retaining walls remain. The Portineria upper garden connection and full upper drive return are unresolved. Neighboring footprints include mapped courtyards and stepped building parts; heights combine OSM information and GlobalBuildingAtlas estimates.

This is a cross-date archive study. Some details and elevations are inferred; exact decoration, hidden circulation and current conditions remain unverified. The regional 5 m DTM is coarse and the absolute floor-to-ground tie remains unknown. Newly found historic 3D CAD elevations are under research and have not changed this model. Enclosure checks do not certify physical weatherproofing or structural performance. [Site data credits](site-data-credits.md).
