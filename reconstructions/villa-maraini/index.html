Research in progress · Rome, Italy

Villa Maraini

Swiss Institute in Rome

A new reconstruction begins with the evidence. We are gathering drawings, photographs and survey records for the villa, its tower and its garden.

Started 8 October 2026 Via Ludovisi 48, Roma 41.906773, 12.487714 · Map ↗

First evidence

  1. A measured survey exists. HMQ documents its 2012 survey for BBL. A reference sheet shows a plan, section, elevation and façade photograph.
  2. A refurbishment competition followed. BBL lists the 2013 jury publication. Proposed changes still need to be checked against what was built.
  3. The building continued to change. Entrance and Sala Elvetica works in 2022–2023 make recent drawings and dated photographs especially useful.
Evidence before geometry

What comes next

Find the full measured survey, reconcile later alterations, and establish scale, floor levels and geographic control.

Architectural/BIM modelling is the initial direction. A Gaussian splat remains an option if suitable overlapping imagery becomes available.

No 3D model is available yet. The first release will follow the evidence review.
