Explore the house

House & capture

The Beatrice von Wattenwyl-Haus at Junkerngasse 59 in Bern’s old town is where the Federal Council receives official guests. This view is a 3D Gaussian splat, trained from the panoramas of the house’s public Matterport tour.

Sharp at capture positions Where the panoramas were taken, rooms look much as photographed.

Soft between them The positions are about 1.7 m apart at one height, so views away from them turn foggy. This is not a measured model.

Move around

Mouse
Drag to look around, double-click a spot to walk there, scroll to move forward or back, right-drag to move sideways.
Touch
Drag with one finger to look around. Double-tap a spot to walk there: the floor to stand on it, a wall or picture to walk up to it. Pinch to step forward or back.
Keyboard
WASD or the arrow keys move, Q and E go down and up, Shift moves faster. F frames the whole house, R returns to the start view.
Orbit
The camera buttons at the bottom right switch between this walking view and orbiting a point: drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click or double-tap to choose the point.

Settings, at the bottom right, switch between high and low quality rendering. Touch screens start in low quality, which keeps phones responsive.
