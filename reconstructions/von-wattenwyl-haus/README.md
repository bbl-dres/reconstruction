# Beatrice von Wattenwyl-Haus

Patrician townhouse in Bern's old town, where the Federal Council receives its guests. A Gaussian splat experiment.

> [!WARNING]
> **Experiment – for viewing only.** Built from publicly available data. Not survey-grade and not accurate: do not use it for measurements, planning or any other purpose.

- **Viewer:** [bbl-dres.github.io/reconstruction/reconstructions/von-wattenwyl-haus](https://bbl-dres.github.io/reconstruction/reconstructions/von-wattenwyl-haus/)
- **Run locally:** `python tools/serve.py --building von-wattenwyl-haus`
- **Made with:** LichtFeld Studio

`public/` is published ([files](public/README.md)); `work/` is the private workspace, gitignored and never published.
