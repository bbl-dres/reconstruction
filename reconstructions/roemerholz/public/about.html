Sammlung Oskar Reinhart «Am Römerholz»

A reconstruction research project for the villa and galleries in Winterthur. Initial sources include BBL's 2010 renovation drawings and the museum's 2026 history and reopening publications.

This viewer shows a first working model (v001): the villa, gallery and linking buildings in the state documented in 2025, with the garden, terrain and surrounding buildings. It is modelled from publicly available data (plans from the Swiss Federal Archives or on request, open geodata), not a measured survey, and is still under review.
