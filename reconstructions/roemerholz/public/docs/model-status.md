# Römerholz: research status

Started and checked: 8 October 2026. Phase 1: evidence gathering. No 3D model or release exists yet.

The [BBL renovation sheet](https://www.bbl.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/J0CtjBU3FYIA/20101001_Winterthur%20Sammlung%20Oskar%20Reinhart%20Am%20R%C3%B6merholz%20Erneuerung_DE.pdf) contains a site drawing, section, ground-floor and basement plans on page 1, plus exterior and interior/construction photography across both pages. The drawings include graphic scale bars. They describe the works completed in October 2010; they have not yet been calibrated or verified against today's building.

The museum's [2026 history publication](https://www.roemerholz.ch/dam/de/sd-web/sfPUnKRRhyGa/2026_Geschichte-der-Sammlung_Deutsch.pdf), page 4, identifies later work by Tropeano Architekten and changes to finishes, lighting, access and garden. [Current visitor information](https://www.roemerholz.ch/de/regulaere-offnungszeiten) confirms reopening on 9 May 2026. The target is provisionally the latest documented condition; older and newer evidence remain distinct.

## Next evidence

- Dimensioned as-built plans, elevations and sections, including upper floors and executed 2025–2026 changes.
- Dated photographs or 360° captures covering each gallery, historic room, exterior face and connection.
- Survey control, roof/terrain data and measured floor levels.
- A targeted local visit after checking photography and capture arrangements with the museum.

Architectural/BIM reconstruction is the initial direction. No suitable public 360° or multi-view training dataset was confirmed in this first search. A Gaussian splat can be reconsidered if overlapping capture and reuse permissions become available.

It identifies Haldenstrasse 95; it is not the reconstruction's surveyed origin or proof that one EGID covers the entire ensemble.
