# Published files – Landgut Lohn

Everything in `public/` is committed and published on GitHub Pages: `building.json` (viewer and pipeline settings), `about.html` (help text), `models/` (catalog and versioned GLB, BIM and IFCZIP assets written by `tools/model-pipeline`), `profiles/` (export profiles), `previews/` and `docs/`. Never place evidence, plans, photos or tour downloads here; they belong in `../work/` (gitignored).

## Published version

Only the latest release is published, to keep the repository small. Earlier versions (v001–v007) remain frozen in the private authoring workspace with their checksums.

| Version | Content | Evidence summary |
|---|---|---|
| v008 | Main house (EG, 1. OG, Dachgeschoss) with the open-well stair, Peristyl, Dependance, terrace and steps; furnished interiors of 17 rooms photographed in 2019; 500 × 500 m surroundings with terrain, land cover, 64 neighbouring buildings, walls, basins, hedges and 830 trees | See [docs/model-status.md](docs/model-status.md) |

Third-party data in the surroundings: swissALTI3D 2025, swissSURFACE3D 2023 and swissBUILDINGS3D 3.0 (3D Tiles 2026-05-20), © swisstopo, open government data (OGD); land cover, walls and basins from the cadastral survey (Amtliche Vermessung, open data); garden areas, hedges and trees of the Landgut parcel from the BBL green inventory (Bundesgärtnerei). The building geometry is derived from non-public BBL plans and archive drawings, a third-party 360° tour and photographs whose rights are not cleared; none of these is redistributed here, only the reconstructed model and renders of it.
