Building & site

Landgut Lohn in Kehrsatz near Bern is the Federal Council's country residence for official guests. The main house was built in 1782/83 for the von Tscharner family to plans attributed to Carl Ahasver von Sinner; a Dependance (former stables, now the dining hall) is linked to it by a glazed Peristyl added in 1959/60. The estate was bequeathed to the Confederation in 1942 (admin.ch). It is renovated from October 2026 to 2029; the model shows the state before that renovation.

About this model

An experimental reconstruction, not a survey. Walls, rooms and openings follow 2005 floor plans; roofs, eaves and chimneys are fitted to the swisstopo swissSURFACE3D 2023 point cloud (median deviation under 0.02 m); storey and ceiling heights and room finishes were measured from the 2019 360° tour of the Swiss Armed Forces' digital media service. Heights of the attic and the Dependance upper floor, window details, furniture and colours are partly inferred and marked as such on every object. Terrain and neighbouring buildings: swissALTI3D and swissBUILDINGS3D, © swisstopo (open government data).
